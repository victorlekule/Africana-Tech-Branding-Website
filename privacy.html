Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of data:

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone, company name, and other details you provide.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, timestamps.
  • Client Project Data: Data processed on your behalf, kept strictly confidential.
02

How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries and deliver services.
  • Manage projects, updates, and invoices.
  • Improve our website and services through analytics.
  • Send marketing communications only with consent.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
03

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share only:

  • Service Providers: Hosting, payments, analytics – under confidentiality.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger or acquisition.
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose.
04

Cookies & Tracking

Our site uses cookies for analytics, functionality, and essential operations. You can control cookies via browser settings.

05

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes collected. Client project data is typically kept for 3 years after completion, then securely purged.

06

Your Rights

  • Access – request a copy of your data
  • Rectification – correct inaccuracies
  • Erasure – ask for deletion under certain conditions
  • Restriction – limit processing temporarily
  • Data Portability – receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection – object to direct marketing or other processing

Contact privacy@africanatech.co.tz to exercise these rights. Response within 30 days.

07

Security

We use encryption, firewalls, secure servers, and regular audits to protect your data. No online transmission is 100% secure, but we continually strive to keep your information safe.

08

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data; if discovered, we will delete it promptly.

09

We may update this policy and will notify you of material changes via email or website notice. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
